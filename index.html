Directed research, Brandeis MSBA, summer 2026

Does a Celtics-specific model beat the score and the clock?

I built a real-time win probability model for the Boston Celtics in Python with XGBoost, using eight seasons of NBA play-by-play data. I tested it against a simpler model that uses only score margin and time remaining, and scored every prediction on a season the model had never seen.

Overall, the two models were a statistical tie. The Celtics model was better calibrated early in games, before the score separates.

  • Rebuilt and checked 636 games of play-by-play data, including every lineup change
  • Found and documented seven defects in the NBA’s historical data feed
  • Built a browser app that replays any game with the model’s probability after every play, and later expanded it to 10 seasons
