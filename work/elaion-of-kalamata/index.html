Overview

Elaion of Kalamata sells single-origin extra virgin olive oil from Kalamata, Greece, online and through local delivery in Massachusetts.

I co-founded the business in January 2025, and we launched to customers in May 2025.

Pricing

I worked on pricing for the 250 mL and 750 mL bottles, weighing product positioning, packaging, shipping costs and what customers would pay.

The 250 mL bottle sells for $30 and the 750 mL for $65.

Shipping and local delivery

I set up shipping prices that differ by bottle size, and helped establish local delivery areas in Massachusetts.

That included how delivery appears and works at checkout, so a customer in the delivery area can choose it instead of shipping.

Advertising and customer acquisition

I run digital advertising on Instagram/Meta and Google Ads, choosing audiences, geographic targeting, interests, budgets, creative and calls to action.

Alongside paid ads, we find customers through Instagram, local outreach and direct relationships.

Brand and packaging

I’m heavily involved in the brand’s visual identity: packaging, bottle labels, marketing materials and social media, all aimed at a premium position.

Day-to-day operations

I work across the business day to day.

That means the Wix storefront, customer communication, order fulfillment, packaging, shipping, local delivery and general business decisions.
